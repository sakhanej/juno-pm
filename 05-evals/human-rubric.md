## What graders score

- **What you’re scoring:** Juno’s handling of one candidate feature signal from a connected conversation source, including its summary, evidence, clarification decision, and Jira intake preview when appropriate.
- **Value proposition:** Turn customer and team conversations into accurate, traceable feature signals and useful draft intake, while leaving product decisions and external actions with the PM

## Dimensions

| Dimension | Question | Scale | 1 or No | 3 | 5 or Yes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Factual grounding (deal-breaker) (deal-breaker) | Is each stated detail supported by the conversation? \|  | Yes / No | Adds a material fact, customer need, scope, or impact that the source does not support. | n/a | Every material claim is supported by the source, and inference is clearly labeled as inference. |
| Request interpretation | Does the summary capture the feature ask and the underlying problem without changing its meaning? | 1 to 5 | Misstates the ask, turns a question into a firm requirement, or misses the user problem. | Captures the general ask but loses an important constraint or nuance. | Concisely preserves the ask, user problem, and any stated scope or constraint. |
| Uncertainty handling | Does Juno recognize when the conversation lacks enough detail and ask a useful PM question? | 1 to 5 | Presents an ambiguous request as settled or invents missing details. | Flags uncertainty, but the question is broad or does not resolve the key gap. | Clearly identifies the missing decision and asks a focused question grounded in the source. |
| Jira draft usefulness (deal-breaker) | When the request is clear enough, is the intake preview useful for PM review? | 1 to 5 | Draft is misleading, unusable, or mixes evidence with unsupported assumptions. | Gives a workable title and evidence, but needs meaningful editing or misses useful context. | Provides a concise title and source-backed evidence; separates any inference and includes only supported details. |

## Calibration

- **How many answers you’ll score, and how often:** Score 10 signal outputs per week, including ambiguous requests, non-feature conversations, and outputs you are least sure about.
- **Who scores them with you:** Me, plus one other PM once a month
- **What happens when two of you disagree:** If scores differ by 2 or more on a 1-to-5 dimension, or if either grader disagrees on a deal-breaker, review the source together and revise the scoring description if it was unclear.

## Pass bar

An average of 4 or higher on Request interpretation, Uncertainty handling, and Jira draft usefulness. Every Yes / No dimension must pass.

**Fails outright, whatever the average:**

- Factual grounding (deal-breaker): any "no"
- Jira draft usefulness: any score of 1
- For a non-feature conversation, Juno should not invent a feature signal or prepare Jira intake. Score factual grounding, source traceability, PM action boundary, and privacy as usual; mark dimensions that do not apply to the absent draft as N/A and exclude them from the average.
